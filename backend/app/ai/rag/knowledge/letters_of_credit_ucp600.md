# Letters of Credit (LC) & Uniform Customs and Practice (UCP 600) Guide

## 1. What is a Documentary Letter of Credit?
A Letter of Credit (LC) is a financial undertaking issued by a bank (the Issuing Bank) on behalf of a buyer (the Applicant) guaranteeing payment to a seller (the Beneficiary) upon presentation of specified documents strictly compliant with the LC terms.
- Letters of Credit operate independently of the underlying sales contract. Banks deal in documents, not goods (Article 4 of UCP 600).

## 2. Standard Types of Letters of Credit
- **Irrevocable LC:** Cannot be amended or cancelled without the consent of all parties (issuing bank, confirming bank, and beneficiary). All LCs under UCP 600 are irrevocable by default.
- **Confirmed LC:** A second bank (usually in the seller's country, the Confirming Bank) adds its independent guarantee of payment, mitigating political and sovereign risk of the issuing bank's jurisdiction.
- **LC at Sight:** Payment is released immediately upon presentation and examination of compliant documents (within maximum 5 banking days under UCP 600 Article 14b).
- **Usance / Deferred Payment LC:** Payment occurs at a predetermined maturity date (e.g., 30, 60, or 90 days from Bill of Lading date).

## 3. Standard Document Checklist for Export LC
Under typical cross-border agricultural commodity contracts, the LC mandates:
1. **Commercial Invoice:** Issued for 100% invoice value, exactly matching the beneficiary and buyer names, currency, and trade description.
2. **Full Set (3/3) Clean "On Board" Ocean Bills of Lading:** Consigned "To Order" and endorsed in blank, marked "Freight Prepaid" (for CIF/CFR) or "Freight Collect" (for FOB).
3. **Insurance Policy or Certificate:** Covering minimum 110% CIF value under Institute Cargo Clauses (A), in same currency as the LC.
4. **Certificate of Origin:** Issued by recognized Chamber of Commerce.
5. **Packing List:** Detailed breakdown of containers, bags, net weight, and gross weight.
6. **Certificate of Analysis / Quality & Weight Inspection:** Issued by independent surveyor (e.g. SGS, Bureau Veritas, Eurofins).
7. **Phytosanitary Certificate:** Issued by national plant protection organization.

## 4. Discrepancies and Handling
If presented documents do not comply with the credit terms (e.g., late shipment date, spelling errors, mismatched weights), the issuing bank issues a **Notice of Refusal** within 5 banking days stating all discrepancies.
- Options:
  1. Beneficiary corrects and resubmits documents before the LC expiry date.
  2. Issuing bank requests applicant (buyer) waiver.
  3. Presentation on collection / approval basis (buyer must explicitly approve before payment release).
