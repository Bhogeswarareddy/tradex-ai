# EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) & Sustainability ESG Due Diligence

## 1. EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR - Regulation (EU) 2023/1115)
The EUDR entered into force to curb greenhouse gas emissions and biodiversity loss driven by agricultural expansion into forested areas.
- **Scope:** Covers coffee, cocoa, palm oil, soya, rubber, wood, and cattle products imported into or traded within the European Union.
- **Core Requirements:**
  1. Products must be **deforestation-free** (produced on land that has not been subject to deforestation after December 31, 2020).
  2. Produced in compliance with relevant local legislation of the country of production (land tenure, environmental regulations, labor rights, indigenous peoples' rights).
  3. Supported by a rigorous electronic **Due Diligence Statement (DDS)** submitted via the European Commission's online TRACES system.

## 2. Geolocation Traceability & Plot Verification
- Importers and upstream traders must collect GPS coordinates of the precise agricultural land plot where each commodity batch was harvested:
  - For land plots under 4 hectares: Single central latitude and longitude coordinate with 6 decimal places.
  - For land plots $\ge 4 \text{ hectares}$: Full polygon boundary coordinates enclosing the harvest perimeter.
- Remote satellite imagery (e.g., Copernicus Sentinel, Global Forest Watch) is cross-referenced with time-stamped harvest logs to verify no tree canopy degradation occurred post-2020.

## 3. Supply Chain Due Diligence & Corporate Sustainability Directives (CSDDD)
The Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) requires large European corporations to identify, prevent, mitigate, and account for human rights and environmental harms across their entire global supply chain tier-1 and tier-2 partners.
- **Mandatory Supplier Audits:**
  - Prohibition of child labor (ILO Convention 138 and 182).
  - Fair living wages, freedom of association, and workplace occupational safety standards.
  - Zero use of banned chemical pesticides (Stockholm and Rotterdam Conventions).
- Supplier documentation must include verified traceability audit reports (e.g., SEDEX SMETA 4-Pillar audit or BSCI social compliance audit).
