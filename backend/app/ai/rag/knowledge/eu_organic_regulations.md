# European Union Organic Farming Regulations (EU 2018/848) & Import Controls

## 1. Regulatory Framework: Regulation (EU) 2018/848
Regulation (EU) 2018/848 is the governing law for organic production and labeling of organic products imported into the European Union.
- Replaced Regulation (EC) No 834/2007.
- Enforces strict compliance approach: third-country operators exporting to the EU must comply with rules equivalent to EU organic production standards.
- Prohibits synthetic fertilizers, genetically modified organisms (GMOs), ionizing radiation, and non-approved crop protection substances.

## 2. Certificate of Inspection (COI) via TRACES NT
All organic consignments entering the European Union must be accompanied by an electronic **Certificate of Inspection (COI)** issued through the European Commission's **TRACES NT** (Trade Control and Expert System New Technology).
- **Issuing Body:** An accredited Control Body / Control Authority recognized by the European Commission (e.g., Control Union, Aditi Organic Certifications, OneCert, Ecocert).
- **Mandatory Procedure:**
  1. Exporter's control body generates COI in TRACES NT before departure from origin port.
  2. The carrier / freight forwarder links COI number to the Bill of Lading.
  3. EU Border Control Post (BCP) inspects documentation and issues endorsement box 30 before releasing customs clearance.
  4. Without a valid electronic COI endorsement, the goods cannot be marketed as organic and must either be downgraded to conventional or re-exported.

## 3. APEDA & Indian Organic Certification (NPOP vs EU Equivalency)
- In India, export of organic products is regulated by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (**APEDA**) under the National Programme for Organic Production (**NPOP**).
- For shipments to Europe, operators must hold certification under **both NPOP and accredited EU 2018/848 scopes**.
- Certified operators receive a unique transaction certificate (TC) per shipment cross-referencing batch numbers and organic acreage.

## 4. Sampling, Ethylene Oxide (ETO) and Maximum Residue Limits (MRL)
- **Ethylene Oxide (ETO) Alert:** The EU has zero-tolerance limits (0.02 - 0.05 mg/kg limit of quantification) for ethylene oxide and its metabolite 2-chloroethanol in spices, sesame, and guar gum.
- Consignments must include certified laboratory test reports from ISO 17025 accredited laboratories (e.g., Eurofins, SGS, Vimta Labs) proving absence of ETO, nicotine, and prohibited pesticide residues.
