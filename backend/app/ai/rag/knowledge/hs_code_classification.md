# Harmonized System (HS) Code Classification for Agricultural Commodities & Spices

## 1. Structure of Harmonized System Codes
The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS) is governed by the World Customs Organization (WCO):
- **First 2 digits:** Chapter (e.g., `09` = Coffee, tea, maté and spices).
- **First 4 digits:** Heading (e.g., `0910` = Ginger, saffron, turmeric (curcuma), thyme, bay leaves, curry and other spices).
- **First 6 digits:** Subheading (international standard, e.g., `0910.30` = Turmeric / curcuma).
- **8 or 10 digits:** National tariff line / TARIC in EU (e.g., `0910.30.00.00`), determining specific tariff rates, anti-dumping duties, and import quotas.

## 2. General Rules for the Interpretation (GRI)
1. **GRI 1:** Classification is determined according to the terms of the headings and relative Section/Chapter notes.
2. **GRI 2:** Incomplete/unfinished goods and mixtures of materials.
3. **GRI 3:** Goods classifiable under two or more headings:
   - 3(a): Most specific description takes precedence.
   - 3(b): Essential character rule for mixtures and composite goods.
   - 3(c): Heading that occurs last in numerical order.
4. **GRI 4:** Goods akin to most similar articles.
5. **GRI 5:** Cases, packaging containers, and packing materials.
6. **GRI 6:** Classification at the subheading level.

## 3. Key HS Classifications in TradeX AI Portfolio
### Spices and Botanicals (Chapter 09)
- **Turmeric (Curcuma):** `0910.30`
  - `0910.30.00.10`: Organic Turmeric Root (whole/sliced)
  - `0910.30.00.90`: Organic Turmeric Powder (curcumin 3% - 5.5%)
- **Ginger:** `0910.11` (neither crushed nor ground), `0910.12` (crushed or ground)
- **Black Pepper (Piper nigrum):** `0904.11` (neither crushed nor ground), `0904.12` (crushed or ground)
- **Cardamom:** `0908.31` (neither crushed nor ground), `0908.32` (crushed or ground)
- **Cumin Seeds:** `0909.31` (neither crushed nor ground), `0909.32` (crushed or ground)
- **Cloves:** `0907.10` (neither crushed nor ground), `0907.20` (crushed or ground)

### Grains, Pulses and Oilseeds (Chapter 10, 11 & 12)
- **Basmati Rice (Semi-milled or wholly milled):** `1006.30.20`
- **Soybeans:** `1201.90` (other than seed for sowing)
- **Sesame Seeds:** `1208.90` or `1207.40` (whether or not broken)
- **Chia Seeds:** `1207.99`

## 4. Tariff Impact & Rules of Origin
- **Tariff Preference:** Goods originating in developing partner countries under GSP or Free Trade Agreements (e.g., India-EU FTA negotiations or India-UAE CEPA) require a valid **Certificate of Origin (COO)** to benefit from preferential tariff rates (0% - 2.5% vs standard MFN rate of 5.5% - 12.8%).
- **Sanitary & Phytosanitary (SPS) Measures:** Specific HS codes trigger automated document inspections on the European TRACES NT portal or US FDA Prior Notice system.
