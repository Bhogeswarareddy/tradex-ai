# International Commercial Terms (Incoterms® 2020) Guide

## 1. Overview of Incoterms 2020
Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) are published by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) and define the responsibilities of buyers and sellers for the delivery of goods under sales contracts. They allocate costs, risks, and operational tasks relating to transportation, customs clearance, and insurance.

Incoterms do not determine ownership transfer, breach remedies, or governing law—they solely address logistics risk and cost allocation.

## 2. Rules for Any Mode or Modes of Transport
### EXW (Ex Works)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Place goods at buyer's disposal at seller's premises (factory/warehouse). Does not load goods onto collecting vehicle or clear for export.
- **Buyer's Responsibility:** Assumes all costs and risks from seller's door to destination, including export clearance, carriage, and import duties.
- **Risk Transfer:** When goods are placed at buyer's disposal at named place.

### FCA (Free Carrier)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Delivers goods to carrier or named party nominated by buyer, cleared for export.
- **Buyer's Responsibility:** Contracts carriage from delivery point and pays subsequent freight and import taxes.
- **Risk Transfer:** When goods are handed over to the buyer's nominated carrier.

### CPT (Carriage Paid To)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Contracts and pays freight to named destination port/terminal, clears export.
- **Buyer's Responsibility:** Import duties and carriage from destination terminal.
- **Risk Transfer:** When goods are handed to the *first carrier*, NOT at destination.

### CIP (Carriage and Insurance Paid To)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Pays freight to destination and procures Institute Cargo Clauses (A) all-risk insurance covering at least 110% of contract value.
- **Risk Transfer:** When delivered to first carrier.

### DAP (Delivered at Place)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Delivers goods ready for unloading at named place of destination.
- **Buyer's Responsibility:** Unloading and import clearance/taxes.
- **Risk Transfer:** Upon arrival at named destination ready for unloading.

### DPU (Delivered at Place Unloaded)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Delivers and *unloads* goods at named terminal or destination place.
- **Risk Transfer:** Once goods are unloaded at the destination.

### DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Maximum seller obligation. Pays all freight, export clearance, import duties, VAT, and delivers to buyer's door.
- **Risk Transfer:** When goods are placed at buyer's disposal at destination cleared for import.

## 3. Rules for Sea and Inland Waterway Transport
### FOB (Free on Board)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Delivers goods on board the vessel nominated by buyer at named port of shipment (e.g. FOB Chennai Port). Clears export.
- **Buyer's Responsibility:** Contracts sea freight, insurance, and pays all costs from ship's rail onward.
- **Risk Transfer:** When goods are safely loaded *on board* the vessel.

### CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Pays sea freight to destination port (e.g. CIF Port of Hamburg) and procures marine insurance (Institute Cargo Clauses C minimum, often ICC A for perishables).
- **Buyer's Responsibility:** Unloading at destination port, import customs clearance, and inland transit.
- **Risk Transfer:** When goods are loaded on board at port of origin, despite seller paying freight to destination.

### CFR (Cost and Freight)
- **Seller's Responsibility:** Pays ocean freight to destination port, cleared for export.
- **Buyer's Responsibility:** Marine insurance and import duties.
- **Risk Transfer:** When goods are loaded on board at port of origin.

## 4. Key Recommendations for Agricultural & Perishable Trade
- For containerized shipments, **FCA** and **CIP** are legally preferable over FOB and CIF because container handoffs occur at inland container depots (ICD) before loading on board.
- When buying CIF, buyers should insist on **Institute Cargo Clauses (A)** to cover temperature variation, moisture, and infestation risks for spices and agro commodities.
