# Marine Cargo Insurance & Institute Cargo Clauses (A, B, C) Guide

## 1. Role of Marine Cargo Insurance in International Trade
Marine cargo insurance indemnifies the cargo owner against physical loss or damage to goods during sea, air, or multimodal transit.
- Under CIF and CIP contracts, the seller is legally obligated to procure insurance for the benefit of the buyer.

## 2. Institute Cargo Clauses (LMA / IUA Standard)
The standard internationally recognized policy wordings are the **Institute Cargo Clauses (ICC)**, published jointly by the Lloyd's Market Association (LMA) and the International Underwriting Association (IUA):

### Institute Cargo Clauses (A) - "All Risks"
- Broadest coverage available.
- Insures against *all risks* of physical loss or damage, except expressly excluded perils.
- Recommended for high-value agricultural commodities, organic spices, and manufactured food items susceptible to rough handling, moisture, or container damage.

### Institute Cargo Clauses (B) - Intermediate Perils
- Covers named perils: fire, explosion, vessel grounding/sinking, overturning of land conveyance, collision, discharge of cargo at port of distress, earthquake, volcanic eruption, lightning, water entry into vessel/container, and total loss of sling/package dropped overboard.

### Institute Cargo Clauses (C) - Major Casualties Only
- Minimum coverage required by CIF Incoterms (unless agreed otherwise).
- Covers only catastrophic perils: fire/explosion, stranding/grounding/capsizing of vessel, collision, discharge at port of distress, jettison, and general average contribution.
- *Does not* cover theft, pilferage, rain/seawater damage, condensation, or washing overboard.

## 3. General Exclusions across ICC Clauses
Standard policies explicitly exclude:
- Willful misconduct of the insured.
- Ordinary leakage, ordinary loss in weight/volume, or wear and tear.
- Insufficiency or unsuitability of packaging.
- Inherent vice or nature of the subject-matter (e.g., spontaneous combustion or natural decay of agricultural produce without temperature malfunction).
- Unseaworthiness of vessel when insured was privy thereto.
- War, strikes, riots, and civil commotions (unless separate Institute War & Strikes Clauses are endorsed).

## 4. General Average Principle
Under the York-Antwerp Rules, a General Average event occurs when a voluntary sacrifice or expenditure is reasonably made in time of peril for the common safety of the maritime adventure (e.g., jettisoning cargo or hiring salvage tugs to free a grounded container ship like the Ever Given).
- All cargo owners aboard the vessel must contribute proportionally to the sacrifice, regardless of whether their specific container was damaged.
- Cargo insurance protects the cargo owner against catastrophic General Average claims and provides salvage guarantees to release cargo holds.
