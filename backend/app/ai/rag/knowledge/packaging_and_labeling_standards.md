# Export Packaging, Palletization & Mandatory Labeling Standards

## 1. Primary & Secondary Packaging for Bulk Spices & Botanicals
- **Multi-Wall Kraft Paper Bags:** Standard export packing for organic turmeric powder, ground pepper, and powdered botanicals.
  - Construction: 3-ply to 4-ply virgin bleached kraft paper with an inner high-density polyethylene (HDPE) or food-grade polyliner (50-80 microns) to preserve moisture barrier and prevent aromatic oil degradation.
  - Standard unit capacity: 25.0 kg net weight.
- **FIBC Big Bags (Jumbo Bags):** Heavy woven polypropylene (PP) bags with PE inner liner for whole roots, pulses, or seed grains.
  - Standard unit capacity: 500 kg or 1,000 kg (1 MT).
  - Equipped with lifting loops, dust-proof seams, and discharge spouts.

## 2. Palletization & ISPM 15 Wood Standards
- **Standard Dimensions:**
  - European standard Euro-pallet (EUR 1): 1,200 mm × 800 mm.
  - ISO / Standard US pallet: 1,200 mm × 1,000 mm.
- **Treatment & Compliance:**
  - All solid wood pallets must undergo heat treatment (HT) at minimum core temperature of 56°C for 30 minutes in accordance with **ISPM 15**.
  - Must bear indelible two-letter ISO country code, manufacturer registration number, and IPPC HT stamp.
  - Pallet loads must be stretch-wrapped with UV-resistant film and secured with edge protectors and plastic corner strapping.

## 3. Mandatory Export Shipping Markings
Every shipping bag or carton must display legible waterproof markings:
1. Exporter / Shipper Name & Origin Country (e.g., "Produced and Exported from India").
2. Consignee Name & Destination Port (e.g., "Hamburg, Germany").
3. Product Description and Commercial Grade (e.g., "Organic Turmeric Powder - Curcumin 5%").
4. Batch / Lot Number for Traceability (e.g., "LOT-SBS-2026-TR89").
5. Crop Year & Date of Processing / Packing.
6. Gross Weight & Net Weight in kilograms.
7. Food Allergen Declarations & Organic Certification Logo with Control Body Code (e.g., "IN-BIO-149").
