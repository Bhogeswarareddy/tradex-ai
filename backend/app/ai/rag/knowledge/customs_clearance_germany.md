# German Customs Clearance (ATLAS) & Port of Hamburg Import Procedures

## 1. Import Gateway: Port of Hamburg (DEHAM)
The Port of Hamburg is Germany's largest seaport and the second-busiest container port in Europe.
- Designated as a vital European Border Control Post (BCP / Grenzkontrollstelle) for food, spices, and organic imports originating from Asia.
- Terminal facilities (CTA - Container Terminal Altenwerder, CTT - Container Terminal Tollerort, Eurogate).

## 2. ATLAS Customs Electronic Filing System
Import declarations in Germany must be transmitted electronically via **ATLAS** (Automatisiertes Tarif- und Lokales Zoll-Abwicklungssystem), administered by the German Federal Customs Authority (Bundeszollamt).
- **EORI Number:** Importers and declarants must have a valid Economic Operators Registration and Identification (EORI) number active within the European Union.
- **Entry Summary Declaration (ENS):** Transmitted into the EU ICS (Import Control System) at least 24 hours prior to container loading at foreign port.

## 3. Valuation, Tariffs, and Import Turnover Tax (Einfuhrumsatzsteuer)
- **Customs Valuation:** Based on the CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) value delivered to the EU external border.
- **Customs Duty (Zoll):** Determined by the 10-digit TARIC code. Many agricultural spices and herbs benefit from 0% - 3.2% duty rates under preferential trade frameworks.
- **Import Turnover Tax (EUSt):** Equivalent to German VAT:
  - Standard rate: 19%
  - Reduced rate: 7% (applicable to basic foodstuffs, organic spices, grains, and agricultural produce).
  - Payable at clearance or deferred using an ATLAS deferment account (Aufschubkonto).

## 4. Phytosanitary & Veterinary Border Inspections (GKS Hamburg)
- Agricultural commodities require clearance through the Hamburg Veterinary and Import Office (Veterinär- und Einfuhrdienst Hamburg-Hafen).
- Pre-notification must be registered via TRACES NT portal (CHED-PP for plant products or CHED-D for specific risk products).
- Physical inspection includes container seal verification, sensory checks for mold/insects, and sampling for pesticide residue analysis.
