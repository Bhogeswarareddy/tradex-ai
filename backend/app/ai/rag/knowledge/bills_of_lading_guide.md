# Ocean Bills of Lading (B/L) & Shipping Documentation Guide

## 1. Legal Nature and Functions of a Bill of Lading
An ocean Bill of Lading serves three fundamental legal functions under international maritime law:
1. **Receipt for Goods:** Evidence that the carrier has received the cargo in described apparent good order and condition.
2. **Document of Title:** Represents constructive possession of the cargo; ownership can be transferred by negotiation/endorsement.
3. **Contract of Carriage:** Evidence of the agreement between the shipper and shipping line (carrier).

## 2. Key Types of Bills of Lading
- **Negotiable (To Order) Bill of Lading:** Issued to the order of a named entity (e.g., "To the order of shipper" or "To order of Issuing Bank"). Ownership is transferred by endorsement on the reverse side. The carrier will only release cargo upon surrender of at least one original endorsed bill of lading.
- **Straight (Non-Negotiable) Bill of Lading:** Consigned directly to a named consignee (e.g., "Speisekammer GmbH"). Cannot be transferred by endorsement.
- **Sea Waybill:** Non-negotiable receipt for cargo where ownership is not transferred by the document. Carrier releases cargo to the consignee upon proof of identity, without requiring surrender of an original document.
- **House B/L vs Master B/L:**
  - **Master Bill of Lading (MBL):** Issued by the ocean vessel operator (e.g. Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd) to the freight forwarder / NVOCC.
  - **House Bill of Lading (HBL):** Issued by the freight forwarder / NVOCC to the exporter.

## 3. Clean vs Claused Bills of Lading
- **Clean Bill of Lading:** Contains no clause or notation expressly declaring a defective condition of the goods or packaging (e.g., "torn bags", "wet stains"). Required for Letter of Credit negotiations under UCP 600 Article 27.
- **Claused / Foul Bill of Lading:** Notes damage or shortfall. Banks will routinely reject claused bills under standard documentary credit terms.

## 4. Surrender and Telex Release
- When cargo arrives at destination port before original physical paper B/Ls arrive by courier, the shipper can request a **Telex Release (Express Release)**:
  - Shipper surrenders all 3 original B/Ls to the carrier at origin port.
  - Carrier cables/emails its destination discharge agent authorizing immediate delivery to consignee without physical presentation of original B/L.
